Privacy Policy for Vortexis

Effective Date: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy governs the privacy terms of our mobile application, Vortexis. It explains what information we collect, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our primary goal is to provide you with a safe and seamless gaming experience. Vortexis does not collect, store, or demand any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your legal name, physical address, phone number, or email address. The core game functions entirely offline or locally on your device.

2. Non-Personal and Technical Data

To improve performance and optimize user experience, we or our third-party partners may automatically collect certain non-personal technical identifiers. This information includes:

  • Device information (model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers).
  • Anonymized gameplay statistics (session duration, levels completed, and crash reports).
  • IP addresses for generalized geographic location targeting (country level).

3. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

The Game may integrate third-party software development kits (SDKs) for ad serving and analytics tracking. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and device advertising identifiers (such as Google AAID or Apple IDFA) to deliver personalized ads. You can find their individual privacy policies here:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads Privacy Policy

4. Children’s Privacy Compliance (COPPA)

We are fully committed to protecting the privacy of young children. Vortexis does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from anyone under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has inadvertently provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete it permanently from our logs.

5. Data Security

Since we do not collect personal data, your privacy risks are inherently minimized. For any anonymized technical logs, we employ industry-standard administrative and electronic protection methods. However, please note that no security infrastructure on the internet is completely impenetrable.

6. International Data Transfer & Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your region, you have specific legal rights over data transmission. Since we utilize standardized third-party networks, you have the right to opt-out of personalized tracking through your mobile device system settings (e.g., "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

7. Amendments to this Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any revisions will be instantly active upon updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this webpage. We recommend checking this page periodically to stay informed.
